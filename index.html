Core measurement logic

Distinctions that prevent false conclusions.

≠

Identity is not attraction

A chosen identity can be meaningful even when attraction patterns are mixed, fluid, uncertain, or differently described.

≠

Attraction is not conduct

What a person experiences, wants, fantasizes about, and does are related variables—not interchangeable evidence.

↻

Lifetime is not current

History matters, but past behavior should not overwrite a person’s present attractions, relationships, or self-understanding.

“The goal is not to discover one hidden truth beneath a person’s sexuality. It is to represent the pattern honestly enough that no single dimension erases the rest.”

Scientific status

Clear about what is known—and what is proposed.

Empirical foundations
Supported component distinctions. Research supports separating identity, attraction, behavior, sex traits, gender, romantic attraction, and change across time.
Integrative architecture
MOSAIC is an original synthesis. The seven-domain organization is conceptually reasoned but requires direct structural testing.
Validation status
Not yet a validated psychological or clinical assessment. Any questionnaire or narrative output should be treated as research-informed self-reflection.
